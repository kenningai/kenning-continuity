# Trademark policy

Kenning Continuity is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. That license deliberately grants **no rights in trademarks** (section 6),
which leaves the question of names unanswered. This file answers it.

This policy is not a restriction on the software. The code is free to use,
modify, and redistribute under Apache-2.0, and nothing here narrows that. What
it governs is the use of Kenning AI's **name**, and it exists for one reason:
the ontology is the work.

## Why a name policy exists for an Apache-2.0 project

The value here is not the Python. It is the model — the type lattice with its
derived parent-walk, the decision that `domain` is a parameter rather than a
property, the placement of epistemic standing in edges rather than in a
`confidence` float, and the principle that every invariant lives in a tool
rather than in an instruction.

That work is given away freely, and Apache-2.0 is the right license for it.
But work given away can be relabelled, and a relabelled ontology is one that no
longer reaches the people who can correct it, extend it, or argue with it. The
attribution requirement in Apache-2.0 section 4 and this policy are the same
commitment seen twice: take the work, keep the thread back to where it came
from.

## The marks

- **Kenning**, **Kenning AI**, **Kenning Continuity**, and any Kenning AI
  logo or wordmark

That is the whole claim, and it is deliberately narrow. A phrase like
"temporal knowledge substrate" *describes* this software rather than
identifying its source — anyone building one is entitled to call it that, and
we claim no rights in the words. What identifies source is **Kenning**.

Nor do we claim the word "kenning" itself. It is an Old Norse and Old English
poetic compound — *whale-road* for the sea, *ring-giver* for a king — and in
Scots it means a glimmer of knowing. Use it in poetry, in linguistics, in a
bakery. The claim is to its use as a mark for software of this kind.

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your software is built on.** "Built on Kenning Continuity",
  "uses kenning-continuity", "compatible with Kenning Continuity" — accurate, factual reference is always
  permitted and always welcome.
- **Redistribute the software unmodified** under its own name, retaining
  `LICENSE` and `NOTICE`.
- **Cite the work** in papers, talks, documentation, and internal
  architecture records. Please do.
- **Discuss, review, criticise, or benchmark it** by name.
- **Run it in production**, commercially or otherwise, modified or not,
  without notifying anyone.

## What requires written permission

- Naming a **fork or derivative work** using the marks, in whole or in part.
- Using the marks in a **company, product, service, package, or domain name**.
- Using the marks in a way that implies **endorsement, affiliation,
  certification, or partnership** where none exists.
- Distributing a **modified version under the original name**. If you change
  the ontology or the invariants, the result is your model, not ours — give it
  your own name and say what it descends from.

## Modified versions

Apache-2.0 section 4 already requires you to carry the `NOTICE` file and to
mark changed files. Do both. Then rename: a modified substrate distributed
under the original name makes Kenning AI answerable for a model it did not
author, and makes your work indistinguishable from ours when someone tries to
trace either.

## Asking, and the standing invitation

Requests are answered, not screened. Write to **contact@kenningai.com**.

The same address is the front door for something we want more than permission
requests. If you have run an accumulating memory system at scale and watched it
fail — degrading as mass grows, or confabulating confidently from the first
day — and you have a view on **why this one is built the way it is**, we
genuinely want to hear from you. That failure mode is the reason for nearly
every design decision in this repository, and the people who have hit it
themselves are the readers whose disagreement is worth the most to us.

There is no form and no funnel. It reaches us directly.
